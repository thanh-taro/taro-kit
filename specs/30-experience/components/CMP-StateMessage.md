## Purpose
A consistent, honest message for empty, no-results, offline, linked and not-found states (PRN-05).

## Anatomy
Icon; title; body; optional primary action.

## Variants & states
| Variant / state | Description |
|---|---|
| empty, no-results, offline, info, not-found | the icon and tone vary |

## Behavior
Always offers the next best action when there is one.

## Accessibility
Polite announcements for changes after the page has loaded.
