# Personas

### PER-01 — Owner (Taro — Nguyen Tri Thanh)
An experienced developer who builds small tools as separate projects and uses them every day for work and life.
- **Goals:** open any tool in about a second from the keyboard; keep favorites and recents; present their work with pride.
- **Frustrations:** ad-heavy utility sites, worry about pasting data into unknown sites, scattered tabs, tools that forget everything.
- **Context:** desktop browser most of the day, with the portal installed as an app; sometimes on a phone.

### PER-02 — Visitor
A peer, recruiter or friend who arrives through a shared link.
- **Goals:** quickly understand who Taro is and what Taro has built; try a tool without signing up.
- **Frustrations:** slow or confusing personal sites, dead links, pages that haven't been updated in years.
- **Context:** any device, usually a first and short visit, often from a link preview in chat or social media.
