# Principles

- **PRN-01** **Portal, not monolith.** Tools are independent projects. The portal lists them, launches them and gets out of the way. Adding a tool means adding one catalog entry.
- **PRN-02** **Keyboard-first, instant.** Every destination can be reached from the keyboard, and opening a known tool takes no more than a search and a confirm.
- **PRN-03** **Private by default.** Nothing about the user leaves the device. There are no accounts, analytics or trackers. The user can export and erase their own data.
- **PRN-04** **Taro talks to you.** The portal acts as Taro: every sentence is Taro speaking, in the first person ("I build…", "I couldn't find that"), directly to the person using it ("you"). Short labels and buttons stay plain. The voice is warm, brief and a little playful, with a recognizable look and honest "last updated" dates.
- **PRN-05** **Honest states.** When something can't work (offline, refused embed, missing tool), say so plainly and offer the next best action. Never show a blank or broken view.
