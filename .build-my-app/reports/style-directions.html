Direction A

Taro Milk Tea

Soft and cozy. Rounded corners, gentle depth and macOS-like calm. The lilac comes from taro, and a milk-tea beige marks favorites and "new". The warmth comes from shape and color, so the chrome can stay quiet.

Direction B

Night Market

Built for the keyboard, with a little glow. Close to Raycast and Linear: crisp edges, tight radii, a violet glow on the selection, and monospace key caps everywhere. Playfulness comes from the wide display face and a mango accent, like taro boba with mango at a night market.

Direction C

Taro Sticker

Tactile and cheeky. Thick outlines, hard offset shadows like stickers on a laptop, a rounded display face, and a pandan-mint second accent. It's the most personal and memorable of the three, and the furthest from Linear's polish.

How to choose

A · Taro Milk Tea
  • Calmest for daily use
  • Closest to macOS
  • Warmth comes through softly
B · Night Market
  • Fastest-feeling, keyboard-first
  • Closest to Raycast and Linear
  • Playful only in the details
C · Taro Sticker
  • Most personal and memorable for visitors
  • Boldest of the three
  • Can feel busy for a daily launcher

Next, the chosen direction becomes design tokens (specs/30-experience/tokens.json). Every text/background pair gets a contrast check against WCAG AA in both themes, then the full screens are designed.

All fonts shown are open-licensed (SIL OFL). The real app will host them itself, so no requests go to third parties (NFR-05).
